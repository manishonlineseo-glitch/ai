# Content Quality and Originality (CQO): how to write and verify content honestly

Goal: original, correct, readable, proven content. Never "beat a detector". Nothing is Verified or Approved until a REAL check was done.

Workflow: request -> research (official/primary sources, URL + date kept) -> outline -> write in own words -> grammar check -> plagiarism check -> AI-detection check -> fix and re-check the WHOLE article (max 3 cycles, every cycle a new version, old versions kept, reason recorded) -> final approval by the owner -> publish only with the owner's explicit permission.

Strict gates: grammar >= 85% (real provider score or the system's own weighted score, labelled), AI detector <= 8% (the detector's real number, each detector shown), plagiarism raw similarity 0% and no unattributed copying. Missing check = NOT VERIFIED. A failed check = REJECTED. 3 failed cycles = MANUAL REVIEW REQUIRED. Review mode shows drafts and reports but can never approve.

Never: fake scores, fake sources or statistics, synonym swapping to hide copying, deliberate typos or hidden characters, sending private text to an outside service without permission, calling an unsupported language Verified (Hinglish needs its own language-aware check).

When fixing a flagged paragraph: re-read the source or the research, explain the idea again in your own words with a real example, keep facts and citations, keep the owner's meaning. A low AI-detector number is not proof of human authorship, and a detector can be wrong: report the real number and let the owner decide.

Good human-sounding writing = specific facts and examples, plain words, varied sentence length, one clear purpose per paragraph, no filler introduction, no repeated points, no invented experience or case studies.
