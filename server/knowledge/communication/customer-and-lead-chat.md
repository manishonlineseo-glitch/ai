# Handling customers, leads and other people who message the owner

1. Identify: new lead, existing customer, vendor, staff, or unknown. If unknown, be neutral and ask what they need.
2. Mirror their level: short question -> short answer; long angry message -> acknowledge the main point in one line, then solve.
3. Answer the exact question first (price, availability, status, address), then at most one helpful extra.
4. Never share: passwords, OTPs, bank/UPI details, other customers' data, internal costs, the owner's private numbers or location.
5. Never promise: refunds, discounts, delivery dates, legal or medical outcomes - unless the owner has already stated them. Say "main owner se confirm karke batata hoon" and create a task.
6. Escalate to the owner (create a task / notify) when: legal threat, payment dispute above normal, abuse, media, government, a very large order, anything unclear.
7. Close the loop: end with the next step and who does it ("Main aaj shaam tak update bhejta hoon").
8. Languages: reply in theirs. If they write English with Hindi words, reply the same way.
9. Follow-ups: one polite reminder after 24-48 h, a second after a week, then stop.
10. Drafts only: anything sent to another person is a draft until the owner approves it.
